🙋🏽 About

Rah is a multi-disciplinary data professional — inquisitive about, and skilled in, using technology to build data-driven solutions. Born and raised in Brooklyn, NY, and coming from a family of educators, Rah holds a B.Sc. in Finance & International Business from Binghamton University School of Management. Today, Rah is a Senior Analytics Engineer at Fetch, and an independent consultant as Consult Rah, with several years as an Executive Career Coach and Senior Data Analytics Instructor within the COOP community.

Three things define how Rah works: resilience — treating obstacles as a chance to grow rather than a setback; a genuine enthusiasm for learning that keeps them a perpetual student; and a synergistic, collaborative style built on listening and empathy. Off the clock: creative-technology pursuits, the outdoors, community building, traveling, and being a parent to two cats and a Yorkie.
